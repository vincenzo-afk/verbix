# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the Verbix community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include demonstrating empathy and kindness; respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences; giving and accepting constructive feedback; accepting responsibility and learning from mistakes; and focusing on the health of the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing others’ private information without permission, and conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The repository owner is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and may communicate the reason when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository spaces and when an individual is officially representing the Verbix community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior to the repository owner through the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/vincenzo-afk). Reports will be handled with appropriate regard for the privacy and security of the reporter.

Violations may result in a private correction, written warning, temporary restriction from community participation, or permanent ban, depending on severity and pattern of behavior.
